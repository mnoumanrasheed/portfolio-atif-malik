## 2. Creative Direction & Visual Decisions ("Quiet Authority")
- **White-First Architectural Canvas:** Replaced dark/black monochrome background with 85–90% pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and subtle off-white (`#F7F9F8`).
- **Typographic Scale:**
  - Display: `Manrope` (Sentence/Title case, natural tracking, bold confidence).
  - Body: `Inter` (16–18px, 1.6–1.75 line height for comfortable editorial reading).
  - Eradicated forced uppercase styling and cyberpunk bracketed tags (`[REF]`, `[VOL. 2026]`, `[HIST]`).
- **Hero Transformation:**
  - Eradicated the CRM-like `Executive Folio` card with its nested product ledger.
  - Implemented an authoritative split: ~58% dominant executive typography and ~42% photo-ready architectural editorial frame with a central `A / M` monogram mark and clear photo-replacement slot.
- **Card Usage Reduced Over 50%:**
  - Section 2 (Leadership Perspective): Replaced 3 boxed cards with an open editorial typographic row.
  - Section 3 (Products): Transformed into alternating feature layouts integrating real honest workflow diagrams.
  - Section 4 (Methodology): Restructured into a clean 4-step horizontal delivery timeline (`Understand`, `Define`, `Pilot`, `Iterate`).
  - Section 6 (360 Technologies): Rendered as an archival foundation with an open client directory index.
  - Section 7 (Collaboration): Replaced repetitive tiles with an intuitive interactive audience pathway selector.
- **Restrained Motion:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger handles graceful section reveals with full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks and context cleanup; Framer Motion powers smooth micro-interactions.
